UK Top 50 Playlist Market Analysis - Student Video Script

1. Introduction
Hello, in this project I analyzed daily UK Top 50 playlist data. The main goal was to understand the structure of the UK music market instead of only looking at popularity.

2. Dataset
The dataset contains date, position, song, artist, popularity, duration, album type, album size, explicit flag and album artwork URL. I first checked the data for missing values and duplicates.

3. Data Cleaning
I converted the date column, removed exact duplicate rows, and kept one record for each date and playlist position. I also standardized artist and album type text.

4. Artist Diversity
I counted artists appearing in the playlist and calculated concentration and diversity measures. This helps show whether the same artists appear repeatedly or whether many artists are represented.

5. Collaboration
I identified collaborations using the ampersand symbol in the artist field. I compared solo and collaborative tracks and created a simple collaboration network.

6. Explicit Content
I compared explicit and clean tracks and also compared the Top 10 with positions 11 to 50.

7. Album Structure
I compared album, single and compilation entries and looked at average album size.

8. Track Duration
I converted milliseconds into minutes and grouped tracks into short, medium and long categories. I also compared duration with popularity.

9. Dashboard
The Streamlit dashboard allows users to select a date range, artist, solo or collaboration tracks, and album type. It then updates the charts and metrics.

10. Limitation and Conclusion
The dataset does not contain artist nationality, so I did not make a domestic versus international claim from this data alone. Overall, the project provides a simple interactive view of artist diversity, collaboration, content and release structure in the UK Top 50.
